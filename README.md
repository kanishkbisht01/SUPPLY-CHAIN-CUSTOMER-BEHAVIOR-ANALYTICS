#  Supply Chain & Customer Behavior Analytics

## Overview

This project presents a comprehensive **end-to-end data analytics solution** built on a real-world supply chain dataset. It combines **sales data, customer information, and web traffic logs** to generate actionable insights that support data-driven business decisions.

The analysis focuses on identifying inefficiencies in delivery, understanding customer behavior, and optimizing overall business performance.

---

##  Business Objectives

* Evaluate **sales performance and profitability** across regions and categories
* Identify **late delivery patterns** and operational bottlenecks
* Analyze **customer segments and purchasing behavior**
* Measure **conversion performance** by linking web traffic with sales
* Develop a **multi-page interactive dashboard** for stakeholders

##  Key Insights

*  **Logistics Issue:** Over 54% of deliveries are delayed
*  **Shipping Inefficiency:** First Class shipping shows ~95% late deliveries
*  **Top Market:** Europe contributes the highest revenue share
*  **Customer Behavior:** Consumer segment dominates overall sales
*  **Conversion Gap:** High product views but relatively low purchase conversion


---

##  Business Recommendations

* Optimize logistics by **reducing dependency on First Class shipping**
* Increase marketing efforts in **low-performing regions (Africa, LATAM)**
* Improve **conversion strategies** for high-traffic products
* Introduce **customer retention programs** for frequent buyers
